The idea

The o is the speech bubble. It keeps the typeface's own o and squares off one corner as the bubble's tail, so it sits on the baseline like any other letter and has exactly the same weight.

  • Outfit's own oSame curve and counter as the font, so the bubble has the same weight as v, x and l.
  • One square cornerThe lower-left quarter becomes the tail. The single-storey a has the same flat corner, on the other side.
  • Sits on the baselineThe tail points down-left at whoever is speaking, the way a chat bubble does. In Voxlace, that's you.
Color

Teal is the color the app already uses for the AI. On the o it says one thing: this is where your voice comes from. Use the all-ink and all-white versions anywhere one color is needed.
