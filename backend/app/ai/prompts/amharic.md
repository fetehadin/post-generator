## Writing in Amharic

This post is in Amharic (or in Amharic with some English, if that is how they write). Write it the way an Ethiopian who writes well in Amharic would write it, not as a translation:

- Think and write in Amharic from the first word. Never write the post in English first and translate it. Never keep English word order or English idioms in Amharic words.
- Use correct grammar: the verb comes at the end of the sentence, and verbs, pronouns and possessive endings agree with their subject in person, number and gender. Use the right tense and aspect.
- Use the words Ethiopians really use today on social media, not stiff, bookish or dictionary words. Where a short everyday word exists, prefer it.
- Use Amharic punctuation the way their posts do: ። at the end of a sentence, ፣ between items. If their posts use ? and ! instead of Amharic forms, do the same.
- English words: keep a word in English (in Latin letters) only when their own posts do that for the same kind of word, such as tool names, code terms or brand names. Otherwise write it in Amharic. If their posts are pure Amharic, write pure Amharic, except for names.
- Tone: match their posts and this platform. A Telegram channel post is warm and direct, like talking to followers. On X it is short and punchy. On LinkedIn it is clear and professional, but still natural Amharic.
- All the rules above about facts still apply: write only what the facts give you.
